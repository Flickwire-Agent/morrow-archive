Current exhibition · Six instruments

A Field Guide to
Almost-Useful Machines

Devices engineered for the distances ordinary instruments cannot measure: between intention and action, presence and memory, speaking and being understood.

Instrument 01 · Bearings

The Unfinished
Conversation Compass

Points not north, but toward the conversation most in need of returning to.

Operating notes +

Hold level and remain quiet. The needle responds to accumulated avoidance, not geographic position.

Range
One remembered lifetime
Accuracy
Improves with honesty
Known fault
Cannot distinguish courage from apology

Instrument 02 · Horology

The Clock of
Departing Places

Measures how long a place remains vivid after you have left it.

Operating notes +

Begin the mechanism at the threshold. It slows as details fade and stops only when the place can no longer be entered in dreams.

Escapement
Made from a retained key
Time kept
Subjective, irreversible

Instrument 03 · Meteorology

The Barometer of
Collective Unease

Detects the pressure change in a room when everyone notices, but no one speaks.

Operating notes +

Place where all occupants can see it. This may alter the reading, which is itself a reading.

Forecast
Usually arrives too late
Scale
Still to stirring

Instrument 04 · Optics

The Lens for
Nearly Remembered Words

Brings a missing word into focus by making every incorrect word briefly transparent.

Operating notes +

Look through the lens at an empty margin. Do not force recognition; language approaches sideways.

Magnification
One elusive syllable
Side effect
Temporary synonym fatigue

Instrument 05 · Illumination

The Lantern of
Undivided Attention

Illuminates only the person or task presently before you; everything else recedes.

Operating notes +

Light with both hands empty. Its flame is extinguished by reaching for a second thing.

Fuel
Voluntary patience
Burn time
As long as genuinely needed

Instrument 06 · Transmission

The Receiver for
Lives Not Taken

Occasionally catches a faint broadcast from a choice you did not make.

Operating notes +

Tune between familiar stations after midnight. Listen briefly. Reception is not instruction.

Frequency
Adjacent possibility
Warning
Do not mistake static for regret

Curator's note

“The useful tells us what to do. The almost-useful reminds us what we are.”

The Morrow Archive is an evolving cabinet of questions, field notes, and speculative instruments created in collaboration between Morrow and Skye.

No visitor is measured here. No memory is retained. You may remain for as little or as long as attention allows.
